Executive Summary & Fluid Mechanics Overview

Plants cultivated in extraterrestrial habitats encounter a physical environment devoid of natural gravity-driven buoyancy ($\text{Gr} \to 0$), expanding unstirred fluid boundary layers around vegetative canopies and drastically elevating aerodynamic resistance ($r_a = 1/g_{bl}$).

This comprehensive 3D Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) investigation compares five distinct spaceflight plant growth systems across four gravitational regimes: Earth (1.0g), Mars (0.38g), Moon (0.166g), and Microgravity (0g).

Evaluated Spaceflight Hardware Platforms

1. NASA VEGGIE (VPS)

Volume: 37.61 L Shoot Zone

Topology: Top Suction Fan ($\varnothing 50\text{ mm}$), 4 Base Intake Slots

Key Finding: 52.8% canopy stagnation under low fan in 0g; 100% spore export to cabin.

2. NASA Advanced Plant Habitat (APH)

Volume: 83.36 L Shoot / 9.44 L SC

Topology: Dual Opposing Lateral Cross-Jets ($0.60\text{ m/s}$)

Key Finding: Invariant $g_{bl} \approx 1.07\text{ mol m}^{-2}\text{s}^{-1}$, 45% displacement ventilation, 0% cabin export (HEPA).

3. NASA CHROMEX / PGU

Volume: 49.57 L Chassis / 0.866 L PGC

Topology: Creeping Flow ($\text{Re} = 123$), Brinkman-Darcy Root Foam

Key Finding: Root hypoxia ($\text{O}_2 < 5\%$) within 35 min explains flight *ADH* upregulation ($14.2\times$–$28.5\times$).

4. CARA Square Dishes ($\pm$ Light)

Volume: $100 \times 100 \times 20\text{ mm}$ ($0.200\text{ L}$)

Mounting: Attached to ISS Cabin Rack Wall

Key Finding: Microgravity boundary layer thickening causes ethylene accumulation ($0.85\text{ ppm}$) and lid condensation (6.5 h).

5. BRIC / BRIC-LED Round Dishes ($\pm$ Light)

Volume: $\varnothing 60 \times 15\text{ mm}$ in PDFU Canisters

Enclosure: Hermetically Sealed (Zero External Flow)

Key Finding: Complete diffusion limitation ($r_{\text{tot}} > 101\text{k s/m}$), extreme hypoxia ($\text{O}_2 < 1.8\%$), ethylene $>3.8\text{ ppm}$.
